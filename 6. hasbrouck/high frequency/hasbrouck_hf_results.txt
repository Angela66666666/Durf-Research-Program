==============================================================================
STEP 4 -- PRICE DISCOVERY AT 1 MINUTE
basket (11 sector ETFs + 8 election adds) vs Kalshi PRES-2024-DJT
==============================================================================
9361 one-minute observations over 24 trading days, regular hours only
weights: daily Jun-Sep Polymarket regression, not re-fitted intraday
error-correction term: z = basket - 0.632 * kalshi
overnight changes never enter any regression below.

PART 1  BASELINE FIT  (all regular-hours minutes, n=9217)
                    alpha         t   adjusts?
  basket        -0.000826     -3.31   YES
  kalshi         0.000317      0.44   no (cannot reject 0)
  Gonzalo-Granger share : basket 27.7%   kalshi 72.3%
  Hasbrouck IS (kalshi) : 98.4%  [98.3%, 98.6%]   residual corr -0.012
  Taken at face value this says KALSHI LEADS. Parts 2-4 test whether
  that survives, and it does not survive in the form stated.

  (for reference, if overnight changes are NOT dropped: residual corr
   +0.395, IS kalshi 77.2% -- the 23 overnight gaps carry the election
   news and swamp one-minute moves, which is why they are excluded.)

PART 2  TEST A -- is the adjustment spread through the day?
  If the basket genuinely tracks Kalshi minute by minute, alpha should
  look similar all day. If it is only catching up after the open, it
  should be concentrated in the morning.

  window               n     a_basket        t     a_kalshi        t
  09:30-10:30       1273    -0.001649    -1.66     0.001049     0.61
  10:30-12:00       2160    -0.001624    -2.89    -0.001229    -0.81
  12:00-14:00       2880    -0.000256    -0.74     0.001140     0.85
  14:00-16:00       2880    -0.000330    -0.94    -0.000090    -0.07
  -> the basket's adjustment is a MORNING phenomenon; from noon onward
     the coefficient falls by roughly five-fold and loses significance.

PART 3  TEST B -- delete the first N minutes of each session
  skip             n     a_basket        t     a_kalshi        t
  0             9217    -0.000826    -3.31     0.000317     0.44
  15            9024    -0.000691    -2.85     0.000353     0.48
  30            8664    -0.000824    -3.49     0.000235     0.31

  120           6504    -0.000223    -0.95     0.000536     0.61
  -> once the first 90 minutes are removed the effect disappears
     (t falls from -3.3 to -1.3). The whole baseline result lives in
     the opening stretch of the session.

PART 4  TEST C -- is the morning move actually AIMED at Kalshi?
  Parts 2-3 locate the effect at the open but do not show the basket is
  chasing Kalshi specifically rather than just being noisy at the open.
  For each session measure the gap at 09:30 and what each market does
  over the following 90 minutes.

  n = 23 sessions
                                                     slope       t       p     R2
  C1 opening gap -> BASKET's next 90 min           -0.1999   -3.04   0.006  0.306
  C2 opening gap -> KALSHI's next 90 min (placebo)    0.0213    0.60   0.553  0.017
  C3 overnight Kalshi move -> BASKET's 90 min       0.0898    1.79   0.088  0.132

  C1 is negative and significant: when the basket opens ABOVE its
     Kalshi-implied level it drifts DOWN, and vice versa. It closes
     about 20% of the opening gap within 90 minutes.
  C2 is the placebo and is flat: Kalshi does NOT move toward the gap.
     This is what rules out 'both are just following a common trend' --
     the adjustment is one-sided.
  C3 points the same way but is only marginal at n=23.

==============================================================================
PART 5  CONCLUSION -- two layers, stated separately
==============================================================================
LAYER 1 -- OVERNIGHT-TO-OPEN. The prediction market absorbs news while
  the equity market is shut, and the basket carries a gap into the open.
  This part is PARTLY MECHANICAL: a closed market cannot respond, so it
  is not by itself evidence about efficiency and is not claimed as such.

LAYER 2 -- HOW FAST THE GAP CLOSES AFTER THE OPEN. This part is
  informative, and it is the actual finding:
    - the basket closes only ~20% of the opening gap over 90 minutes
      (C1: slope -0.200, p=0.006, R2=0.31);
    - the prediction market does not adjust at all (C2: p=0.55);
    - after roughly 90 minutes NEITHER side error-corrects, so there is
      no detectable intraday lead in either direction (Parts 2 and 3).

  In words: sector ETFs absorb prediction-market information with a lag
  concentrated in the first ~90 minutes of trading, and only partially;
  once that opening adjustment is done, neither market leads intraday.

  What must NOT be claimed: that Kalshi leads the ETF basket minute by
  minute through the session. The ~98% information share in Part 1 is an
  artefact of the opening window and is reported only to be refuted.

LIMITATIONS
  - Parts 4's tests are session-level with n=23; they are suggestive, not
    decisive, and C3 is only marginal.
  - The 90-minute window overlaps generic opening price discovery, which
    cannot be fully separated from election-specific adjustment.
  - The 1-minute residual cointegration test is borderline (ADF t = -2.04
    vs 5% critical near -2.86); the spread does mean-revert with a
    ~1.2-1.7 trading-day half-life on both the 1-minute and daily fits, so
    the cointegrating vector is treated as imposed prior information from
    the daily estimation rather than as a fresh rejection.
  - Regular hours only: the overnight channel itself is not measured.
  - The basket spans only part of the election factor (level correlation
    ~0.57), so every statement is about THIS basket.
